BriefLoop initialization panel assets
=====================================

The BriefLoop initialization panel adapts interaction-layout and visual-system
source from the official PPT Master repository:
https://github.com/hugohe3/ppt-master

The adaptation is pinned to PPT Master commit
619a954695d866dde970552db9fb1a6640c643c8. The adapted upstream source surface
is skills/ppt-master/scripts/confirm_ui/.

for its initialization panel; the upstream ownership and license notice below
